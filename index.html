01 — About

Comfortable on either side of the dashboard.

At 19, I started a food business out of pure curiosity about whether people would pay for what I was making — they did, and it taught me more about pricing, demand, and operations than any course could.

Since then I've worked the finance side, building KPI dashboards and running variance analysis and forecasting, and the growth side, modeling attribution, analyzing funnels and tracking campaign ROI — often on the same team, sometimes in the same week. That range is on purpose: I'd rather stay useful across both than specialize too early into just one.

I graduated from Waterloo and I'm actively figuring out what's next. Wherever that lands, the habit stays the same — find the signal in the noise, then actually do something about it.
